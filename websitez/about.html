Our Story

Built For Gathering & Good Coffee

A Community Space Designed For Work, Study, And Relaxation

Bukluran Cafe opened its doors in Luzviminda 2, Dasmariñas, Cavite with a clear vision: serving high-grade coffee offerings without premium price tags.

The name Bukluran stems from unity. Every corner provides comfortable seating, accessible power outlets, reliable Wi-Fi, and a relaxed ambiance for students, workers, and coffee lovers.
